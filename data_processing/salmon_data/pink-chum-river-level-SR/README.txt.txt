# Assembly of river-level spawner-recruitment datasets for wild salmon populations in British Columbia

Contact: Emma Atkinson \<ematkinson@ualberta.ca\>

## Overview 

This repository contains the data, code, and documentation for the assembly of spawner-recruitment (S-R) data for wild pink and chum salmon populations in British Columbia (excluding the Fraser River watershed). The data are assembled at a river- or system-level rather than an aggregate spatial scale. These data are assembled for the analysis described in the publication, "Cumulative effects of forest harvest on Pacific salmon in coastal British Columbia" by M.D. Hocking and M. Kuruvilla et al. 

## Repository Structure

The analysis for this paper draws on two compilations of river-level spawner-recruitment data for two parts of the BC coast. The Pacific Salmon Foundation compiled river-level S-R data for pink, chum, coho, and sockeye populations within the North and Central Coast region (DFO Pacific Management Areas 1-10). For this paper, Ecofish (now Trinity Consultants) drew on the data compiled by PSF and further compiled river-level S-R data for pink and chum populations in the South Coast Vancouver Island & Mainland Inlets region (DFO Pacific Management Areas 11-29). 

The data and code for these two compilations is organised in two folders: 

`\2019-NCC-SR-assembly` contains materials (inputs, code, outputs) for previously assembled river-level S-R data by PSF for the North & Central Coast.

`\Ecofish-SCVI-assembly` contains materials (inputs, code, outputs) for assembling the coastwide S-R data, including the output from the NCC S-R assembly combined with S-R data for the south coast assembled drawing on data from PSF and DFO. 

**Note:** Some datasets are too large to post on GitHub (see .gitignore file); please contact Emma to request these.
